Speed decoupling — position decoding from neural activity, speed, or both

Three position decoders, one per column group, identical in everything but their input, so every comparison is paired:

  • MLP (Optuna, 20 trials; 5 trial-wise outer folds × 1 validation hold-out)
  • Same kept bins (post-context tail removed at 515° ≈ 62.9 cm, Gaussian smoothing σ = 200 ms, no history window)
  • Same trial folds

Experiments:

  • Neural Act. (Batch 007): smoothed spike counts of the kept neurons in the current 200 ms bin, plus the trial's context one-hot (rewarded / unrewarded).
  • Speed (smoothed) (Batch 013): no neural data. One speed value: the continuous raw speed series smoothed with exactly the Gaussian applied to the spike counts (σ = 200 ms, same function), sampled at the current bin. Speed and neurons then share the same temporal transformation, whereas the window also exposes acceleration. The same context one-hot is appended (3 inputs).
  • Neural Act. + Speed (smoothed) (Batch 014): the Neural Act. features (without their context columns) plus the 3 Speed (smoothed) inputs.

Values are medians over the 5 outer test folds: R² and mean absolute error (MAE, cm). Summary rows are medians of the per-recording values.
